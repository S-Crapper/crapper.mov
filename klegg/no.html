link your discord and stoat community with majic klegg

what klegg does

  • klegg's wizard automatically clones channels, roles, emoji, and permissions from discord to stoat in one pass
  • discord webhooks paired with stoat's masquerade feature keep avatars and usernames consistant on both sides

setup
